## Well, ...it's like Ladies' Night, but _better_

### Looking for something to do?

Head Uptown to spend the day!

_Per artist walk-in availability, we'll be starting our **NEW WOMEN'S WEDNESDAYS** promotion at BluTattoo this month! We'll have light refreshments provided by local restaurants, and as always, we'll put the kettle on and have the coffee pot full._

- Wanna bring your bestie for matching tattoos?
- Wanna snag unique flash from your favorite artist?
- Looking for a unique gift?
- Plan for a **WEDNESDAY** and get a discounted rate!
- Plus, **FREE light refreshments**!

If you're a locally owned small business and you're interested in providing promotional food or drinks, please contact us!

Afterward, we encourage you to check out the other locally owned businesses and restaurants within easy walking distance of our studio!
